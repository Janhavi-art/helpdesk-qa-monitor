# Connected to Wi-Fi but No Internet

## Symptoms / task

Check whether other websites and applications also fail. Confirm that other devices on the same network can access the internet. Disconnect and reconnect to the network, then restart the computer if needed.

## Recommended steps

If all devices are affected, there may be a network or internet service outage. If only your device is affected, report the exact error and whether the issue occurs on other approved networks. Do not change managed network settings without permission.
