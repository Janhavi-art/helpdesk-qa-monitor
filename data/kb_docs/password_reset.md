# Password Reset and Sign-in Troubleshooting

Status: Generic starter guidance. Use your organization's approved identity process.

## Symptoms
A user has forgotten a password, cannot sign in, or receives a password-related error.

## Steps
1. Confirm that the username is correct and that Caps Lock or the keyboard layout is not causing an issue.
2. Use the official password-reset page provided by your organization. If you do not know the official address, navigate through the company intranet or contact IT rather than following an unknown link.
3. Complete only the identity-verification steps shown by the trusted company process.
4. Create a new password that complies with company policy and retry sign-in.

## Security and escalation
Never share a password, recovery code, or MFA code with support staff. Contact IT if the account is locked, the registered recovery method is unavailable, or the reset process fails. Include the exact error message, but omit secrets.
