# Laptop Will Not Turn On

## Symptoms / task

Check that the power adapter is connected securely and that the wall outlet works. Look for visible damage to the cable or adapter; do not use damaged equipment. Follow the device manufacturer’s documented power-reset steps only if appropriate for the model.

## Recommended steps

If the laptop still does not turn on, contact IT support and provide the model and any indicator-light behavior. Do not open the device or attempt repairs unless authorized.
