# Computer Is Overheating or Fan Is Loud

## Symptoms / task

Keep air vents clear and place the computer on a firm surface. Save work and close unnecessary intensive applications. Do not block ventilation openings or use liquids to clean the device.

## Recommended steps

If the computer becomes unusually hot, shuts down, or makes unusual noises repeatedly, stop using it if it appears unsafe and contact IT support. Do not open the chassis unless authorized.
