# Laptop Not Turning On

## When to Use This Guide

Use this guide when a company laptop does not turn on or shows no response when the power button is pressed.

## Check the Power Supply

1. Connect the laptop to its original charger.
2. Make sure the charger is connected securely to the laptop.
3. Check that the power outlet is working.
4. Leave the laptop connected to the charger for at least 15 minutes.

## Try a Power Reset

1. Disconnect the charger.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds.
3. Connect the charger again.
4. Press the power button normally.

## Check for Signs of Power

Look for power indicator lights, keyboard lights, fan noise, or a display on the screen.

## If the Laptop Still Does Not Turn On

Contact the IT Helpdesk if the laptop remains unresponsive after charging and performing a power reset.
