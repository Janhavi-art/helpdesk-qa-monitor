# Laptop Will Not Turn On

Status: Generic starter guidance. Follow manufacturer and organizational safety instructions.

## Safe checks
1. Connect the laptop to its compatible charger and check that both ends are seated securely.
2. Try a known-working outlet if safe to do so.
3. Inspect the cable and adapter for visible damage. Do not use damaged equipment.
4. Leave the device connected briefly and check for charging or power indicators.
5. Disconnect nonessential accessories and try powering on again.

## Escalate
If the laptop remains unresponsive, becomes unusually hot, smells burnt, or shows battery swelling, stop using it and contact IT. Do not open the device or remove an internal battery unless an authorized technician instructs you to. Include the device model, power-light behavior, charger status, and recent events.
